# Commercial application procedure

Use this procedure only when the request names `opportunity_revenue_leaks`, `opportunity_absence_gaps` or `opportunity_event_packages`. Finish after exactly these steps:

1. Call that one tool once with exactly the arguments given in the request. Do not delegate and do not call other tools.
2. Write the report (at most 250 words, one line per item):
   - **Recommendation**: the returned `headline`, unchanged.
   - **Key figures**: the most important RM figures, ranges and counts, copied exactly from the result with their labels (exposure, indicative, low/mid/high).
   - **Evidence**: two or three returned metrics that support it (sites, incidents, complaints, serviceability, capacity, readiness).
   - **Risks and limits**: rejected or conditional options with their failed conditions, missing evidence, and the returned caveats.
   - **Next business action**: the returned next action, recommended action or proposal step.
3. Never state an RM amount, price, cost or percentage that is not in the result, never call exposure "lost revenue", never call indicative prices quotes, and never promise a service level. All data is synthetic.
